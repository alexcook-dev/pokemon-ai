## Archetype & win condition

Hydrapple ex is the primary attacker. Its attack, Syrup Storm, deals 30 damage per
Grass Energy **in play on your side of the board** (not just energy attached to the
active), doubled to 60/Energy when Meganium is in play — unlike Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's
Myriad Leaf Shower, which only counts Energy attached directly to Ogerpon and the
opposing Active. That difference is the deck's whole thesis: Hydrapple reaches lethal
OHKO numbers on big ex targets (e.g. a 320 HP Dragapult ex) off ~5 total Grass Energy
spread across the board, which is reachable in one turn via multiple attachment sources
(manual attach, Ripening Charge-style effects, Teal Dance), whereas Ogerpon alone needs
4 Energy stacked specifically on itself over 2+ turns to hit the same number. Win
condition: race the prize trade via repeated large OHKOs, using Ogerpon as an earlier
secondary attacker and Hydrapple as the turn where the opponent's board gets erased.

## Game plan by phase

- Setup: develop the Meganium line (Chikorita → Bayleef → Meganium) and the Hydrapple
  line (Applin → Dipplin → Hydrapple ex) in parallel; Meowth ex, Bug Catching Set, and
  Poké Pad are the deck's search/setup package per the real decklist.
- Build: stack Grass Energy broadly across the board rather than concentrating it on one
  attacker — Syrup Storm counts all in-play Grass Energy, so spread attachments count
  toward the OHKO number even before Hydrapple itself is active. Forest of Vitality
  (4 copies) is the Stadium supporting this ramp.
- Attack: Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Myriad Leaf Shower as an earlier/cheaper attacker
  (scales with the opponent's own attached Energy too, so it's better vs targets that
  already committed Energy to their Active); switch to Hydrapple ex's Syrup Storm as the
  primary attacker once Meganium is down and ~5 Grass Energy are in play, for the
  format's big single OHKO turn.

## Key decision rules

- WHEN Meganium is in play AND you need to choose an attacker → prefer Hydrapple ex's
  Syrup Storm over Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Myriad Leaf Shower once total in-play Grass
  Energy (not just Energy on the attacker) reaches ~5, since Meganium's doubling makes
  Syrup Storm reach lethal on 320 HP ex targets faster than stacking Energy on Ogerpon
  directly.
- WHEN the opponent's Active already has 2+ Energy attached AND Ogerpon has any Grass
  Energy on it → Myriad Leaf Shower is comparatively cheap that turn (it counts the
  opponent's attached Energy too) — don't wait for Hydrapple if this alone reaches KO
  range.
- WHEN both Boss's Orders and Unfair Stamp are live in hand with a lethal or near-lethal
  attacker ready → the guide explicitly calls this combo "unfair": gust the priority
  target into range, then lock with Unfair Stamp to deny the opponent's answer.

## Matchups

- vs Dragapult ex: favored per the guide — this matchup is the deck's stated reason to
  exist. Hydrapple ex OHKOs a fully-loaded 320 HP Dragapult ex off ~5 total Grass Energy
  in play (with Meganium), which is reachable faster than Teal Mask Ogerpon ex alone
  reaching the same number (needs 4 Energy stacked specifically on itself). Guide notes
  Dragapult was running as high as a 40% metagame share at time of writing.
- vs Cynthia's Garchomp ex, N's Zoroark ex, Marnie's Grimmsnarl ex, Mega Absol ex:
  guide states these are current top-format decks and are Grass-weak, giving Hydrapple's
  attackers an automatic Weakness bonus toward OHKOs. This is the intro-level claim only
  — the guide's dedicated matchup pages for these (538-742 words each) are paywalled, so
  no turn-by-turn plan is recorded here.
- vs Gardevoir ex / Gholdengo ex: guide notes these were the prior format's top decks and
  are now rotated out — not a current metagame concern per the guide (dated context,
  re-check if the ban/rotation list has changed since ingestion).
- All ~24 other named matchups (Alakazam, Arboliva, Beedrill, Crustle, every Dragapult
  variant besides base Dragapult, Festival Lead, Hide N' Sneak, Hop's Trevenant, the
  Hydrapple mirror, Lillie's Clefairy Box, Mega Excadrill, Mega Greninja, Mega Lopunny

## Tech cards & why

- Unfair Stamp (1×) + Boss's Orders (2×): guide explicitly calls out this combo — a
  big OHKO wall (330 HP Hydrapple ex) buys the time to land Boss's Orders + Unfair Stamp
  and lock the opponent out of their answer, compensating for the format having no
  Iono-level hand disruption.
- Tapu Bulu (1×), Fezandipiti ex (1×), Celebi (1×), Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking (1×),
  Lana's Aid (1×): present in the real winning list but their specific role isn't
  explained anywhere in the free section — recorded as inferred-from-decklist-only, not
  guide reasoning. Don't treat these as confirmed tech rationale.

## POLICY HINTS (testable hypotheses for /ptcg-train)

- H1: When scoring which attacker to power up, weight TOTAL Grass Energy in play across
  your whole board (not just Energy attached to the active/attacker) — because
  Hydrapple ex's Syrup Storm scores off all in-play Grass Energy, a policy that only
  counts Energy-on-attacker will under-value Hydrapple as an attacker choice and delay
  its OHK Os. Expected effect: more turns where Hydrapple attacks as soon as the
  board-wide Energy count supports lethal, instead of waiting to stack Energy onto it
  directly.
- H2: When Meganium is in play, double the scored damage-per-Grass-Energy for Grass
  attackers before comparing attacker options — expected effect: earlier switch to an
  all-in attacking line instead of continuing to develop, since the lethal-Energy
  threshold drops by half.
- H3: When the opponent's Active or a key Benched Pokémon lists Grass as its printed
  Weakness (confirmed by the guide for Cynthia's Garchomp ex, N's Zoroark ex, Marnie's
  Grimmsnarl ex, Mega Absol ex), prioritize attacking into it this turn over further
  bench development — expected effect: higher prize-race efficiency specifically against
  those matchups, without needing full matchup-specific logic.
- H4: When both Boss's Orders and Unfair Stamp are in hand and the board has a lethal or
  near-lethal attacker ready, sequence Unfair Stamp immediately after the KO rather than
  holding it — expected effect: fewer opponent recovery turns after a big Hydrapple/
  Ogerpon OHKO.
